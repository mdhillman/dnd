# Kala

Commonly known as The Unravelling, The Wild Spark, or The Final Entropist, Kala represents the universal force of decay, chaos, and the inevitable breakdown of all structures. She is the energy that turns stone to dust, empires to ruins, and life to compost. While Oriana seeks to pull the universe into a static point and Daesis strives to build it into a perfect machine, Kala is the reminder that no system is closed and no order is permanent. She is not a god of "evil" in the traditional sense, but of the necessary destruction that makes room for new, unpredictable growth.

In the cosmology of the Octad, Kala is the shadow that follows every creation. She is the personification of Entropy—the natural tendency of the universe to move from order into disorder. To her followers, the world is a stale, rigid cage that only she can unlock. She is the patron of the revolutionary, the scavenger, and the terminal patient, embodying the philosophy that beauty is found only in the fleeting, the frayed, and the broken. While other gods may offer stability, Kala offers the liberation of the void, ensuring that the "Great Crunch" of Oriana never happens because the universe simply falls apart first.

The murals found in the overgrown ruins of the Spire and the hidden "Scavenger-Shrines" of the DMZ depict Kala as a figure of manic, unbridled energy.

* **Appearance**: She is depicted as a wild-eyed and energetic young girl, appearing to be in her early teens. Her expression is one of mischievous delight, as if she is witnessing a hilarious cosmic prank. Her eyes are wide and heterochromatic, shimmering with the iridescent colors of an oil slick or a rotting leaf.

* **Attributes**: She is frequently shown dancing atop a crumbling throne or a heap of rusted machinery. In her hands, she carries a Loom of Frayed Threads, which she is perpetually unweaving, and a Dagger of Corrosive Glass that causes anything it touches to instantly tarnish or rot.

* **Attire**: Kala is clad in a chaotic patchwork of frayed silks, rusted chainmail, and salvaged rags. Her clothing seems to be in a constant state of shedding threads and falling apart, only to be replaced by new fragments of debris. She often wears necklaces made of broken gears, cracked gems, and bleached finger-bones.

## Worship and clergy

Clerics of Kala are known as Unravellers, Dust-Walkers, or Entropy-Priests. They are a nomadic and often misunderstood order of scavengers, terminators, and nihilistic philosophers. They are rarely welcomed in the polished halls of New Mallorea or the rigid castles of Doriath, instead dwelling in the fringes of society where things have already begun to fail.

Worship of Kala involves acts of "Creative Destruction," such as the dismantling of abandoned buildings, the burning of outdated laws, or the celebration of the "First Rust." Rituals are often loud, discordant, and spontaneous, involving the breaking of pottery or the scattering of ashes. Her temples are never built from scratch; they are established in places of natural decay—sunken ruins, stagnant marshes, or the "Dead Zones" of the northern borders. To Kala, a completed cathedral is an insult; her holiest sites are those where the roof has caved in and the vines have begun to reclaim the altar.
