# Nihilus

Commonly known as The Silence, The Unspoken, or The Final Witness, Nihilus represents the absolute stillness of death and the crushing weight of kept secrets. He is the quiet at the end of a life and the darkness between the stars where information goes to vanish. While Vitae celebrates the noisy chaos of growth and Kala revels in the messy process of decay, Nihilus is the state of total, immutable cessation. He is the patron of the spy, the mortician, and the archivist, embodying the belief that the ultimate truth of the universe is found only when all sound and motion have ceased.

In the cosmology of the Octad, Nihilus is the ultimate repository. He is the god of The Void - the conceptual space where the energy of Zareos and the matter of Daesis eventually come to rest. To his followers, speech is a leak of power and a distraction from reality. He is the keeper of "The Great Ledger," a mythical record of every word never spoken and every deed done in total darkness. He does not demand the flamboyant destruction of Kala; he simply waits for the inevitable moment when every flame flickers out, claiming the remains in a cold, eternal embrace that preserves them exactly as they were at the moment of their passing.

The austere glass-work in the Catacombs of Port Sinter and the private shrines of the Intelligence Directorates portray Nihilus as a figure of haunting, minimalist presence.

* **Appearance**: He is depicted as a pale, unnervingly thin man with long, slender fingers and sharp, angular features. His face is perpetually fixed in a neutral, unreadable expression, and his eyes are solid pools of matte grey, devoid of any reflection or pupil.

* **Attributes**: He is frequently shown holding a Black Quill made of shadow-glass and a Locked Tome that can never be opened. He is often surrounded by a "hush-aura," a visual distortion in mosaics that suggests sound is being pulled into his form like light into a black hole.

* **Attire**: Nihilus is clad in voluminous, layered robes of ash-grey silk that seem to absorb the light around them. The fabric hangs heavy and motionless, even in high winds. He wears a high, stiff collar that partially obscures his jaw, and a silver mask often hangs from his belt, depicting a face with a sewn-shut mouth.

# Worship and clergy

Clerics of Nihilus are known as Keepers of the Secret, Silent Brothers, or Grey Mourners. They are a deeply private, ascetic order of spies, librarians, and executors. In many cities, they serve as the official "Final Witnesses," tasked with recording the last words of the dying and ensuring their secrets are carried to the grave.

Worship of Nihilus is conducted in total, absolute silence. Rituals involve the "Vow of the Still Tongue," where initiates may go years without speaking a single word. Devotion is shown through the collection and protection of sensitive information, or the meticulous preparation of the dead to ensure they are "ready for the Silence." His temples are never found on the surface; they are deep, sound-proofed vaults, abandoned libraries, or windowless chambers beneath the busiest streets. To Nihilus, a loud prayer is a profanity; his holiest sites are those where even a heartbeat feels like a thunderclap, and where the air is heavy with the static of a thousand years of unshared truths.
