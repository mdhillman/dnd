# Vitae

Often called The Bloom, The Life Giver, or The Eternal Matriarch, Vitae represents the vibrant, pulsing force of biological existence. She is the energy that drives the seed to sprout, the heart to beat, and the forest to reclaim the wasteland. While Daesis builds with cold iron and Kala unravels into dust, Vitae is the exuberant, messy complexity of organic life. She is the patron of the farmer, the healer, and the mother, embodying the belief that the universe is not a machine or a mathematical proof, but a garden that must be tended with love and ferocity.

In the cosmology of the Octad, Vitae is the primary counterbalance to the stagnation of Oriana. She is the force of Vitality—the inexplicable spark that turns raw matter into sentient being. To her followers, reality is a constant cycle of renewal and abundance. She does not care for the rigid logic of New Mallorea; to Vitae, a single wildflower growing through a crack in a laboratory floor is a victory of more significance than any industrial breakthrough. She is the source of the "Great Breath," the cosmic rhythm of inhalation and exhalation that sustains the natural world, ensuring that even in the face of decay, life will inevitably find a way to begin again.

The vibrant tapestries in the Gardens of Glimmermere and the sun-drenched shrines of Andelain depict Vitae as a figure of warmth and boundless hospitality.

* **Appearance**: She is depicted as a friendly, robust older woman with a radiant, sun-kissed complexion. Her face is lined with "laugh-wrinkles," and her eyes sparkle with a deep, emerald-green light. Her hair is a thick, silver mane intertwined with living ivy, blooming jasmine, and ripening berries that never wither.

* **Attributes**: She is often shown carrying an Overflowing Cornucopia that spills forth an endless variety of fruits and grains, and a Staff of Living Willow that buds and blossoms wherever it touches the ground. Birds and small animals are frequently depicted nesting in the folds of her garments.

* **Attire**: Vitae is clad in flowing, earthen-toned robes made of woven flax and silk, embroidered with the lifecycle of the "Sun-Wheat." She wears a heavy mantle of moss and clover that feels warm to the touch and smells of rain-drenched earth.

# Worship and clergy

Clerics of Vitae are known as Bloom-Tenders, Heart-Singers, or Life-Bringers. They are a jovial, nurturing order of midwives, herbalists, and druidic scholars. They are most commonly found in the agricultural heartlands of Andelain and the frontier settlements of the Bronze Union, where they ensure the health of both the land and its people.

Worship involves acts of cultivation, healing, and communal celebration. "The Sowing" is a sacred ritual where priests and commoners alike plant new life in areas scarred by the Re-Indexing War. Rituals are sensory-rich experiences involving the sharing of bread, the scent of crushed herbs, and rhythmic chanting that mimics the sound of a heartbeat. Her temples are rarely enclosed structures; they are open-air groves, lush glass-domed arboretums, or sacred springs surrounded by ancient, towering trees. To Vitae, the greatest sacrilege is the sterilization of nature; her holiest sites are those where the boundary between the "civilized" world and the wild growth of the earth has completely dissolved.
