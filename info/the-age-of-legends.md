# The Age of Legends
The Age of Legends (also known as the Era of First Light or the Pre-Stasis Harmony) is the earliest recorded epoch of Theian history, existing thousands of years before the current agent. It was a period characterized by the physical presence of the [Octad](info?content=gods) on the mortal plane, technological wonders that far exceed contemporary steam-power, and a fundamental understanding of magic that treated the weave as a predictable, benevolent science rather than a chaotic hazard.

## Characteristics of the Era
In this age, the division between the divine and the mundane was practically non-existent. Spirits of the land, air, and sea—such as the Glimmer-Fey of the Aether or the Deep-Earth Sentinels—dwelt openly among the mortal races.

* The Living Cities: Urban centers were not built of brick and mortar, but grown from "Resonant Glass" and "Sun-Iron." Cities like Aethelgard (the fabled predecessor to [Revelstone](info?content=revelstone)) were said to be powered by the literal laughter of the gods, requiring no fuel or manual labor.
* Mastery of the Weave: Magic was not a bureaucratic nightmare policed by [T.H.U.M.P.](info?content=thump), but a universal utility. Even the most common citizen could allegedly manipulate the Realmatic Flux to heat their home or transport goods across the continent in the blink of an eye.
* The High Peace: Without the scarcity of the modern era, war was a concept relegated to poetry and sport. The Dwarves, Elves, Humans, and Orcs lived in a state of high co-operation, guided by the direct wisdom of the higher gods.

## Divine Interaction
The gods of the Octad were not distant icons sequestered in their High Sanctum, but active participants in society.

* [Zareos](info?content=zareos): Known then as the Great Lamp-Lighter, he did not demand the violent conversion of energy through volcanoes, but instead acted as the primary power source for the world's industry, igniting the "Eternal Engines" of the northern empires.
* [Sylaris](info?content=sylaris): In his role as the River Lord, he maintained a perfectly synchronized global timeline. It is whispered that during this age, no one ever arrived late to a meeting, and the very concept of "losing time" was physically impossible.
* [Beldar](info?content=beldar): Provided a "Boundless Canvas" that allowed for the colonization of floating islands and sub-oceanic domes, providing breathable air, warm temperatures, and fertile soil across the planet.

## Notable Regions and Spirits

A number of wonderous regions and places were home to all kinds of spirits during the age. Spirits could live with the mortal populace in a balanced symbiosis, understanding the mysteries of the weave, working the land, and building great new technologies together. Some of these notable works and the spirits that helped make them were:

* The Glass Gardens of Pando: A country-sized botanical preserve where every plant was a semi-sentient spirit. It was said that the flowers sang in perfect harmony with the movements of the stars, a feat that contemporary bards like [Lyro Nox](info?content=lyro-nox) still attempt to emulate.
* The Chronometric Towers of Aethelgard: Long before the current age of clockwork and steam, the city of Aethelgard stood as a testament to the mastery of Sylaris. These towers served as the central hub for the global timeline, ensuring that every second across the continent was identical.
* Arch-Spirit Valerius: A high-spirit of [Oriana](info?content=oriana) who served as the "Master of Tides." He was responsible for ensuring the oceans moved with a predictable, mathematical grace, preventing the chaotic maritime disasters common in the current age.
* The Pyre-Archons: Massive spirits of living flame that managed the Sun-Iron Foundries of Zareos.

## The End of the Legend
While modern scholars in [The Kindred Concord](info?content=nations) debate the exact cause of the era's conclusion, it is universally agreed that the Age of Legends ended with [The Breaking](info?content=the-breaking). The transition was so absolute that nearly all technology from this time was rendered inert, higher knowledge and expertise in the weave lost to time, and the gods' physical forms vanished from the world, leaving behind only [The Spire](info?content=geography) and a strange legacy of "sticky kitchen implements" that were once powerful artifacts.
