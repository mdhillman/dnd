# Thaddeus T Thorne

Thaddeus "The Fucker" Thorne is the serving Commander of the [Tactical Hex Unit & Magical Policing (T.H.U.M.P)](/info?content=thump) organisation, a specialized division of city law enforcement tasked with high-risk arcane containment in major Theian metropolitan centers.

Known for his imposing physical presence, profound cynicism regarding the arcane arts, and a near-supernatural ability to ignore magical effects through sheer stubbornness, Thorne is widely credited with codifying the modern doctrine of "Percussive Counterspelling". He is rarely seen without his signature cigar or wearing anything other than his regulation, lead-lined field plate armor.


## Early Life and career

Little is known of Thorne's early life, though [Revelstone](info?content=revelstone) City Hall records suggest he was born in the industrial district of Port Sinter in [Camorr](info?content=camorr) to a family of quarry workers. He briefly worked as a cobblestone layer before enlisting in the standard City Watch at age 20.

Thorne's early career as a beat cop was unremarkable, save for a record-setting number of internal disciplinary complaints filed against him for "Accidental destruction of public property while issuing a citation". Thorne struggled with standard police procedure, frequently breaking quills due to grip strength issues and inadvertently pulling the doors off hinges when knocking on suspect's homes.

His career trajectory changed during the "Vanderratz Tower Incident" of 1384 NA. While senior mages debated how to penetrate a rogue wizard's Globe of Invulnerability, then-Sergeant Grout grew impatient, leading a small team of Warforged & Trolls, simply walked through the barrier (later claiming he "didn't notice it"), and introduced some "structural instability" to the tower, toppling it with the wizard still inside.

## Formation of T.H.U.M.P.

Following the Vanderratz incident, a number of local city councils recognized the need for a dedicated unit of officers possessing high physical density and low imaginative capabilities to combat escalating magical crime. Thorne was immediately promoted and tasked with forming the unit that would become T.H.U.M.P. Thorne single-handedly wrote the T.H.U.M.P Field Manual. The manual is notable for being only four pages long, three of which are diagrams on how to properly brace a door-ram.

He established the department's core philosophy: "Magic is just physics that hasn't been hit hard enough yet." Under his leadership, T.H.U.M.P shifted focus from arcane investigation to rapid, kinetic deployment, arguing that by the time a detective figures out which demon is being summoned, Thorne's team could have already demolished the summoning circle and arrested the cultists for zoning violations.

## Leadership style and methodology

Commander Thorne is described by subordinates as a "load-bearing fixture" of the precinct. He runs the department from behind a desk fortified with decades of unresolved paperwork, cracked coffee mugs, and trophies of broken wands and staves.

He is known for his profound distrust of anything "sparkly." He refuses to utilize magical equipment, insisting that his standard-issue plate armor—which he reportedly sleeps in—is sufficient protection against "wizard nonsense". Thorne's personal theory on magic is that if you simply refuse to acknowledge a spell is happening, it eventually gets embarrassed and stops working.

His administrative style is blunt. Performance reviews usually consist of the Commander staring at an officer until they apologize for existing, then stamping their file with "ADEQUATE".

## Notable incidents

Throughout his 35-year career, Thorne has personally overseen hundreds of high-profile magical arrests.

* The Great Slime Insurrection (1391 NA): When the city sewers were animated by a druidic eco-terrorist, Thorne led the charge armed only with two snow shovels duct-taped together. The cleanup took three weeks.
* The Invisible Stalker Case (1399 NA): Thorne solved the case in twenty minutes by throwing a fifty-pound sack of flour into an empty room, followed immediately by throwing a chair at the resulting white silhouette.
* The Lich of [Liversgate]() (1402 NA): Thorne arrested a powerful undead spellcaster on charges of tax evasion. When the Lich attempted to use "Power Word: Kill", Thorne simply puffed on his cigar and issued an additional fine for smoking in a non-designated area, confusing the Lich long enough for it to be manacled.


## Personal Equipment

Thorne is never seen without "The Gavel," a massive, dwarven-forged adamantine breaching maul that leans against his office wall. While many assume it is magical due to the sheer devastation it causes, the Commander insists it is just "very heavy." He claims it is used primarily for "opening statements" during raids.
