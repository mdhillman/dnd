# Organisations & groups

While nations squabble over borders and kings debate the divine right of the Octad, the world is truly greased, fueled, and occasionally held hostage by its trans-national organizations. These guilds and collectives operate with their own laws, their own currencies, and - in the case of the more eccentric ones - their own questionable health and safety standards.

## The Industrial & Mercantile Titans

### The Cog-and-Hammer Consortium

Originally a loose collective of Dwarven nomadic smiths and Human factory owners, the Consortium now holds a monopoly on the refinement of "Mirror-Glass." They are the primary worshippers of Daesis and believe that if a problem can’t be solved with a steam-piston, it hasn't been hit hard enough.

They are famous for their "Aggressive Upgrading" policy. Membership requires you to let them "optimize" your household appliances, which often results in toasters that can accidentally calculate orbital trajectories.

### The Silk & Shadow Syndicate

A multi-racial trade guild that handles everything from the distribution of fine Tabaxi-inspired textiles (synthetic, of course) to the high-stakes illicit information brokerage of Port Sinter. They are the only organization that has successfully negotiated trade routes through both the Kindred Concord and the fringes of New Mallorea.

Despite their sleek image, they are the world's primary providers of legal insurance. If you get stepped on by a Mallorean Logic-Legionnaire, the Syndicate’s lawyers will be there before the ringing in your ears stops.

## The Arcane & Academic Orders

### The Unravellers’ Salvage Collective

A "guild" only in the loosest sense, this group is comprised of devout followers of Kala. They specialize in the "de-construction" of hazardous magical sites and the recycling of rusted industrial husks. They view themselves as environmentalists; the rest of the world views them as well-organized looters with a worrying amount of explosives.

Their headquarters is a massive, walking fortress made entirely of scrap metal that is technically "falling apart" at all times. They have a lost-and-found department that is essentially just a giant pile of things they haven't figured out how to break yet.

### The Archivists of the Unspoken

A clandestine network of Nihilus worshippers who operate as the world's "Neutral Memory." They maintain the Great Vaults - subterranean libraries where secrets too dangerous to be known are stored in absolute silence. They are the go-to for high-level witness protection and the "forgetting" of inconvenient scandals.

They have an incredibly popular side-hustle: Ghost-writing anonymous memoirs for disgraced politicians.

## Service & Security Guilds

### T.H.U.M.P

[T.H.U.M.P](info?content=thump), Tactical Hex Unit & Magical Policing (though often colloquially referred to as "The Heavies", "The Door Knockers", or "Oh Gods No, Not Them"), is a specialized, high-impact sub-division of municipal law enforcement forces in major metropolitan centers across Theia, most notably cities with high magical activity like [Revelstone](info?content=revelstone) or Lyonhall. The organisation is lead by [Commander Thaddeus T Thorne](/dnd/info?content=thaddeus-thorne), who is widely credited with codifying the modern doctrine of "Percussive Counterspelling".

### The Blood-Blades

Founded by Orcish war-chiefs to provide honorable employment for their kin, the Aegis is the premier mercenary and private security firm in the world. They operate on a strict code of honor. If they fail to protect a client, the commanding officer must perform a "Song of Apology" that can last for three days.

They are surprisingly talented gardeners. Many Orcish garrisons double as award-winning botanical sanctuaries, a side effect of their secret devotion to Vitae’s "ferociously growing" philosophy.

### The Hearth-Mother's Guild

Don't let the name fool you. These followers of Vitae are the world’s most powerful logistical network. They control the grain silos, the medicinal herb trade, and the "Midwife-Messengers." Because they are welcomed in every home and hovel, they possess the most comprehensive map of the world’s population.

They are terrifying. Even the most hardened Mallorean commander will hesitate to cross a Hearth-Mother, largely because they know all of his childhood embarrassments.

## The Odd & Unexpected

### The Order of the Recalibrated

A small but growing organization of Malloreans and Goblins who have "opted out" of their respective societies. They believe that Logic and Chaos are two sides of the same gear. They spend their time trying to build "Perfectly Random Machines".

They are responsible for the invention of the world's most frustrating sport: Substance-Ball, where the rules change every time the ball hits the ground, determined by a small, onboard logic-core.

### The Whispers

As discussed in recent security briefings, the Whispers have transitioned from a religious sect of Mallorean extremists into a global terror cell. They target "Logic-Anomalies" (civilians) to prove that the world is inherently flawed and needs Mallorean "Correction."

They are known for their impeccable paperwork. Victims of their attacks often find a neatly filed "Notice of Deletion" left at the scene, citing the exact statistical reasons why their neighborhood was deemed inefficient.
