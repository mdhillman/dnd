# Doriath

Doriath is the steely, traditional heart of the Kindred Concord. It is a kingdom bound by ancient oaths, deep forests, and the unyielding duty of the shield. Doriath maintains the alliance's most contentious land borders, particularly against the remnants of New Mallorea, and provides the elite scouts and rangers that form the backbone of the Revelstone Militia. While often at odds with Camorr’s smoky industrialism, Doriath’s commitment to the "Old Oaths" makes them the alliance’s most loyal, if traditional, defenders.

## Geography and Major Settlements

### Arendia

The capital and heart of the realm. Arendia is not merely a castle, but a massive castle-city, meticulously carved into a colossal granite formation rising from the center of the country. Every structure, from the market squares to the High King's keep, is fortified. The architecture seamlessly blends the stark strength of stone with massive, woven living timber, grown from ancient trees and hardened to iron.

### Last Watch

Perched on the jagged cliffs overlooking the northeastern border, Last Watch is a grim defense castle. It is the first line of defense against any potential Mallorean incursion. Its massive siege-ballistae are manned 24 hours a day by Boundary Sentinels, veterans hand-picked for their resistance to both arcane manipulation and sheer boredom. Reports from Last Watch are often categorized into "Mundane" and "Metaphysical," as the proximity to the Spire makes reality unpredictable.

### Riva

Located on the western coast, where the Great Greenwood meets the sea, Riva is Doriath's primary maritime trade hub. While a bustling port, it lacks the chaotic nature of Camorr's harbors. The city is defined by high wooden walls and elegant, ship-shaped buildings. Rivan marines are famously trained to fight both on the open sea and in the flooded mangroves that protect the docks.

## Notable Figures

* **High King Aros II**: The current ruler of Doriath and the commander of the Sylvan Hosts. A stern man, known for his prowess in both leadership and combat, he is the embodiment of the "Old Oaths." While he has publicly disagreed with High Arbiter Alistair Greatblood on the merits of rapid expansion, his loyalty to the Concord is absolute.

* **Captain "Iron-Bough" Morgenstern**: The commanding officer of Last Watch and a war-hero from the Re-Indexing War. Kael is famous for successfully defending the fortress against a Mallorean force that attempted to use a reality-anchor spell to "zoning-permit" the castle into collapsing. He is a master of asymmetric warfare in both forested and magically unstable terrain.

* **Arch-Ranger Vesper**: The realm's leading arcane scout. Vesper is specialized in navigating and "charting" the chaotic reality of the Spire Exclusion Zone. They are known for their signature "Phase-Cloak," which, it's whispered, was salvaged from a Mallorean Logic Legion contingent that got too close to the Spire's chaotic heart. Vesper is the Dorathian liaison to T.H.U.M.P. on matters of anomalous terrain.

## Economy and Culture

Doriath's economy is focused on defense, high-quality resources, and specialized magical items. Arcane knowledge is rare but potent. "Camorr builds with smoke; Andelain builds with dreams. We build with duty, and when the chaos comes, it is our duty that will keep the other two from being indexed." — Aros II, High King of Doriath.

* **The Greenwood Lodge**: This guild controls all logging and forestry in the realm. Their woodcutters are also trained militia, and their most vital task is the harvesting of "Iron-Oak," a tree that grows only in Doriath and is as strong as steel. The Lodge also produces the famous Dorathian Longbows, which require years of training to master. The Lodge holds a trade monopoly on raw materials, often causing diplomatic friction with Camorrian guildhouses.

* **The Wardens of the Old Oath**: More an order than a guild, the Wardens are the elite protectors of Doriath. They are master scouts, trackers, and monster-hunters. The order also maintains the "Strictly No Loitering" policy around the Spire. While they are a military order, they also control the trade of specialized, magically-treated leather and alchemical remedies derived from rare forest herbs.

* **The Sylvan Trade Pact**: While the Concord Sovereign is used for major trade, local Dorathian communities still utilize a system of "Trade Sticks." These are carved wooden tokens, each a complex, unique key, that represent services or goods. A single, intricately carved "Hunter’s Chit" might be traded for enough firewood to last a winter.
