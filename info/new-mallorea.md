# New Mallorea

New Mallorea (officially the Restored Dominion of Mallorea) is the isolated, rival power to the [Kindred Concord](/info?content=the-kindred-concord). Following its devastating defeat in the [Re-Indexing War](/info?content=war-of-unsolicited-reindexing) the remnants of the old empire retreated behind the impenetrable borders of the then-newly imposed Demilitarized Zone (DMZ). Shielded from the world by a ring of "No Man's Land" and a self-imposed vow of indifference with the outside world, New Mallorea has become a "black box" nation. It rejects all diplomacy, bars all visitors, and exists in a state of perpetual, cold defiance against the modern world.

## Geography and the DMZ

New Mallorea is geographically severed from the rest of the continent, locked within a wasteland of its own making. The nation is encircled by a miles-wide area of blasted earth and ruins of long forgotten settlements. The Concord maintains a "Fierce Watch" along the southern rim, manned by Dorathian rangers, Andelainian mages, and Camorrian ironclads out at sea. This DMZ is the most militarized border in history; anything attempting to cross from the Mallorean side is engaged with lethal force.

### Mal Camat

Rumored to be the only surviving megacity within the wastes. While no outsider has seen it in decades, long-range magical scrying and high-altitude thermal imaging suggest a masterpiece of desert engineering, where sandstone citadels have been retrofitted with churning iron gears and massive cooling vents to combat the relentless heat. Every district is organized with a geometric precision that mirrors the nation’s obsession with logic, and the horizon is perpetually dominated by the "Soot-Spires"—massive chimneys that vent the exhaust of the underground foundries where the nation’s hidden war-machine is being meticulously forged.

### Mal Gemil

Located in the scorched southwest near the tension-filled border of the DMZ, Mal Gemil serves as the primary garrison and logistics hub for the Logic Legions. It is a city of low, reinforced bunkers and sprawling training grounds, built to withstand both the natural sandstorms of the region and any potential arcane "spillover" from the Spire's influence. The city is defined by its Signal Towers, which maintain a constant, coded light-relay with the capital, ensuring that the border remains under a state of total, unblinking surveillance.

### The Valley of Bones

To the north lies the Valley of Bones, a vast, wind-swept graveyard of the Old Empire that remains a site of grim archaeological and arcane significance. This desolate landscape is choked with the bleached ribs of ancient "Great-Beasts" and the rusted husks of colossi that fell during the Re-Indexing War. While the area is officially a restricted military zone, rumors persist that the Restoration Assembly (a division of the Logic Legions) is conducting "Necro-Industrial" experiments here, attempting to re-animate the metallic and skeletal remains of the past to bolster the armies of the present.

## Notable Figures

Since no official records have left the country in nearly 200 years, these figures are based on intercepted transmissions and refugee whispers:

* **The Silent Emperor**: Intelligence suggests a figurehead has finally been placed on the throne, possibly a genetic scion of the old bloodline. Rumors claim he hasn't spoken a word since his coronation, communicating only through "Directive Scrolls" delivered by masked attendants.

* **Inquisitor Rhuarc**: Rhuarc is the spiritual architect of the "New Order" in New Mallorea. He is not a traditional priest, but the head of the Cult of the Perfect Prime, a religion that treats mathematical logic as the only true divinity and the Old Empire as its lost, perfect paradise.

* **Moghedien the Spider**: is the header of the Whispers, New Mallorea’s clandestine intelligence and sabotage wing. While Inquisitor Rhuarc screams his defiance from the spires of Mal Camat, Moghedien is the one ensuring that when the time comes, the Concord’s gates will be unlocked from the inside.

## Economy and Culture

The most terrifying development for the Concord is the rumor that New Mallorea has abandoned its post-war "Rationalism" and returned to the dark, expansionist philosophies of the Old Empire. "We built a cage and called it a DMZ. But when you look into a cage for hundreds of years and see nothing but shadows, you start to wonder if you're the one being kept out, or if the thing inside is just waiting for the lock to rust." — High King Aros II, Private Journal.

* **The Logic Legions**: While the Concord has its wardens, mages, and rangers, New Mallorea has the Legions. They are reported to be "Reality-Anchor" specialists who operate in the grey spaces of the DMZ. They do not hunt monsters; they hunt "Inconsistencies." Sightings of these soldiers—clad in featureless grey plate—near the Doriathian border have caused widespread panic in recent months.

* **The Restoration Assembly**: Believed to be the primary industrial guild. They are tasked with the "Great Reordering" - a project rumored to be the total reconstruction of the Old Mallorean war machine. If the rumors of their success are true, New Mallorea may be reaching industrial parity with Camorr without ever having traded a single ounce of gold.

* **The Whispers**: The clandestine street-cells and sleeper agents under Moghedien command, have undergone a chilling evolution, shifting from subtle psychological subversion to overt, calculated brutality. No longer content with sabotaging machinery or stalling bureaucracy, these cells have begun orchestrating high-casualty "Calibration Strikes" against civilian hubs.

* **The "Old Oaths" Perverted** : While Doriath follows Oaths of protection, New Mallorea is rumored to have reinstated the Oaths of Reclamation, religious decrees that state the entire continent is technically Mallorean property currently being "mismanaged" by squatters.
