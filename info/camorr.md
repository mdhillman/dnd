# Camorr

Camorr (officially the Lyon-Led Republic of Camorr) is the industrial and maritime heart of the Kindred Concord. A nation defined by grand architecture, soot-stained factories, and a "gentlemanly" approach to both trade and crime. Camorr controls the vast majority of the southern continent's shipping lanes and steam-technology patents. While officially a member of the Concord, Camorr is a land where the law of the ledger often carries more weight than the law of the gods.

## Geography and Major Settlements

### Lyonhall

The seat of government and the largest city in the country. Lyonhall is a city of sharp contrasts, dominated by a massive ancient castle that dates back to the early Age of Dust. The castle serves as the primary residence for the ruling Lyon family, while the surrounding city is a dense sprawl of marble townhouses, smog-belching foundries, and the central offices of the nation's merchant princes.

### Port Sinter

Located on the east coast, Port Sinter is the primary gateway for international trade—and a notorious hive of criminal activity. It is a city of mist and salt, where the legitimate "Concord Sovereign" trade is constantly shadowed by a thriving black market. It is widely known that the local authorities are often on the payroll of the city's powerful "Trade Syndicates."

### Fort Kandor

A heavily fortified and meticulously staffed stronghold on the northern border. Fort Kandor serves as Camorr's primary watchtower against New Mallorean aggression. The garrison here is trained specifically to spot the "Census Legions" and is equipped with the latest long-range steam-cannons.

### Woodhelven

A world-famous architectural marvel situated on, and sprawling across, the border with Andelain. Woodhelven is a vertical, tree-top village built into the massive, magically-resilient timber of the border forests. During the War of the Unsolicited Re-Indexing, it served as a vital strategic holdout, as its shifting elevations made it a logistical nightmare for Mallorean surveyors.

## Notable Figures

* **Lord Steffan Lyon**: The current High Lord and ruler of Camorr. A shrewd political veteran, he is the primary architect of Camorr’s modern industrial boom and a firm ally of Alistair Greatblood in the Great Council.

* **Cygnus "The Chaste" Lyon**: The High Lord’s young son, whose nickname is a masterpiece of Camorrian irony. Cygnus is notoriously promiscuous and a permanent fixture in the scandal-sheets of the court, well-known to the younger male & female socialites of Lyonhall for his charm and his utter lack of interest in "chastity".

* **Don Lorenzo Salvara**: The current Don of The Tridents, a powerful criminal mafia. While Lord Lyon handles the official trade, Salvara manages the illicit movement of goods across Camorr. The Tridents are rumored to have a reach that extends even into the Halls of Consensus, specializing in artifacts that T.H.U.M.P. would rather see destroyed.

* **General "Iron-Side" Kaelen**: A veteran of the [War of the Unsolicited Re-Indexing](/info?content=war-of-unsolicted-reindexing), Kaelen is the man credited with the successful defense of Fort Kandor.

* **Arch-Alchemist Vespera Thorne**: The estranged elder sister of [Commander Thaddeus Thorne](/info?content=thaddeus-thorne), Vespera is considered the most brilliant (and dangerous) mind in the field of Steam-Arcana.

## Economy and Culture

The Camorrian culture is built on the concept of the "Honest Lie." In Camorr, it is understood that everyone has a motive, and as long as that motive is profitable, it is respected. "In Camorr, you can buy anything - a law, a lineage, or a very fast boat. Just make sure you get a receipt; the only thing more dangerous than a Camorrian thief is a Camorrian auditor" — Common traveler's proverb.

* **The Steam-Guilds**: Camorr’s middle class is dominated by engineers and artificers who maintain the nation’s complex network of steam-pipes and rail-lines.

* **The "Gentleman's Game"**: Politics in Lyonhall is often conducted through elaborate balls and high-stakes gambling, where a single hand of cards can determine the tax rate of an entire province.

* **The Masked Tradition**: Mirroring the aesthetic of Port Sinter, many Camorrian nobles wear elaborate porcelain masks during public functions, a habit born from a desire to remain "un-indexed" during the Mallorean wars.
