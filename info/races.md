# Races of Theia

Following the cataclysmic events of [The Breaking](/info?content=the-breaking) thousands of years ago, the various races of the world were severed from their ancestral planes. What remains is a diverse tapestry of peoples who have adapted to the unique pressures of the New Age, from the industrial smog of Camorr to the sterile logic-halls of New Mallorea.

## The Common Races

* **Humans**: Humans are the undisputed backbone of the Kindred Concord and regularly found across most of the continent. Lacking the innate magical longevity of the Elves or the rigid biological focus of the Malloreans, Humans rely on their sheer tenacity and curiosity. They are the primary drivers of the Industrial Revolution, possessing a unique ability to adapt to any climate - from the marshy reaches of Sea Reach to the high-altitude spires of Doriath. Their culture is one of rapid expansion, often acting as the diplomatic "glue" that holds the alliance together.

* **Elves**: The Elves of this age are far removed from the flighty forest-dwellers of myth. They are a somber, secretive people who live by a strict societal structure known as the Three Castes.
  * Warrior Caste: Protectors of the ancient groves and elite members of the Doriathian military. They value honor and martial discipline above all.
  * Worker Caste: The architects and master-growers. They are responsible for the "living buildings" of Woodhelven and the intricate irrigation of Glimmermere.
  * Religious Caste: The keepers of the "Old Oaths." They serve as the spiritual advisors to the High Druid and maintain the crumbling shrines of the Age of Legends.
  
* **Dwarves**: In a departure from traditional subterranean tropes, the Dwarves are a nomadic, hardy people primarily found within the Bronze Union. Whether traversing the scorching deserts in sand-skiffs or navigating the treacherous swamps in iron-clad barges, they are defined by their devotion to Daesis, the God of the Forge and the Path. For a Dwarf, a crafted object is not just a tool, but a prayer made manifest; they carry their workshops with them, trading masterwork gear for the supplies needed to continue their endless pilgrimage.

* **Orcs**: Orcs are a proud, militaristic race with a culture deeply rooted in personal and familial honor. Modeled after the great warrior traditions of old, they value strength, loyalty, and the "Song of Battle." Unfortunately, because of their imposing stature and historical roles as conquerors, they face systemic prejudice within the Concord. This has led many Orcs to form self-sufficient, fortified communities (Sects) where they can practice their traditions—such as the Rite of the Blood-Blade—away from the suspicious eyes of Human bureaucracy.

* **Malloreans**: The Malloreans are a race apart, appearing as tall, slender humanoids with skin in shades of cerulean and cool grey. Biologically, they are built for the sterile, hyper-logical environment of Mal Camat. Malloreans possess an emotional baseline that is significantly more dampened than other races, allowing them to process complex mathematical and arcane data without the "interference" of passion or fear. They are remarkably long-lived and require less sleep than Humans, often spending their "rest cycles" in a state of meditative calculation.

## Minor races & the "Gate-Stranded"

Because access to other planes was disabled millennia ago, races like Tieflings, Aasimar, and Genasi are vanishingly rare. They are not "extra-planar" themselves, but rather the descendants of immigrants who were trapped here when the gates slammed shut.

  * **Tieflings and Aasimar**: Often seen as living artifacts, their celestial or fiendish traits have diluted over generations, appearing now as subtle shimmering eyes or faint metallic skin-sheens.
  
  * **Halflings and Gnomes**: These races have largely integrated into Human or Elven societies. Halflings are often the premier logistical managers of Millford, while Gnomes provide the frantic, experimental spark in Camorr's most dangerous laboratories.

  * **Goblins**: In the smog-choked alleys of Camorr and the deep mines of Andelain, Goblins are the "grease in the gears" of the world. Far from the primitive raiders of folklore, Theian Goblins are obsessively clever, possessing an intuitive grasp of mechanical failure and chemical volatility. They are the primary labor force in the most hazardous sectors

  * **Dragonborn**: The Dragonborn are a race of living history, believed to be the artificial creations of the Age of Legend's greatest mages. Without a homeland of their own, they serve as the ultimate mercenaries and bodyguards across the Concord. They are defined by a nomadic "Legacy-Debt," a cultural belief that they must earn their right to exist through deeds of immense martial or magical significance.

  * **Tebaxi**: The Tabaxi are the tragic ghosts of Theia. Once a thriving civilization of traders and scholars from the outer jungles, they were nearly hunted to extinction during the "Century of Pelts", a dark period where their fine, magically-resonant fur became the ultimate fashion statement for the decadent elite of the Old Mallorean Empire.

  * **Goliaths**: At the highest mountain peaks — far above the slopes where trees grow and where the air is thin and the frigid winds howl — dwell the reclusive goliaths. Few folk can claim to have seen a goliath, and fewer still can claim friendship with them. Goliaths wander a bleak realm of rock, wind, and cold. Their bodies look as if they are carved from mountain stone and give them great physical power. Their spirits take after the wandering wind, making them nomads who wander from peak to peak. Their hearts are infused with the cold regard of their frigid realm, leaving each goliath with the responsibility to earn a place in the tribe or die trying.
