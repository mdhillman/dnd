# The Breaking

The Breaking (also referred to as The Great Revision, The Silence, or The Age of Iron and Dust) was a catastrophic, era-defining period of global upheaval that ended the [Age of Legends](info?content=the-age-of-legends). It marked the transition from a world of divine harmony and high technology to one of regressive survival, eventually resulting in technology and magical understanding revert to near-primitive states. While the exact cause remains a subject of intense academic debate and theological mourning, the physical and metaphysical scars of the event define the geography and magical instability of modern Theia.

The most widely accepted academic theory within [The Kindred Concord](info?content=nations) suggests that The Breaking was the result of a "Great War" of unimaginable scale. This conflict supposedly involved every major mortal race and the spirits of the land, utilizing weapons of Sun-Iron infused with the power of high-tier Realmatic Flux that effectively "scorched the air and blocked out the stars". Following the peak of the conflict, the [Octad](info?content=gods) withdrew their physical presence from the mortal plane, "slamming the celestial doors" and leaving humanity and its kin to navigate the fallout alone.

The infrastructure of the Age of Legends - including the Living Cities and Eternal Engines ceased to function as the gods' power was withdrawn, forcing the survivors into a crude, technologically inept existence.

## Legacy 
The most enduring physical legacy of The Breaking is the [Spire](info?content=geography) at the center of the continent. Rather than a natural mountain, it is viewed by magical scholars as a "cosmic stitch" designed to hold a fractured reality together. The persistent magical static surrounding the Spire is believed to be the "radiation" left over from whatever event occurred during the Breaking.

Beneath the historical "Great War" narrative, fringe theorists and certain un-rememberable entities whisper that the Breaking was actually a civil war among the gods themselves, resulting in a division so deep it was literally reflected on the face of Theia.

## Long-Forgotten Regions

The Ash-Fields of Solrathis: Once a lush garden-province of [Oriana](info?content=oriana), it was transformed into a landscape of obsidian glass and choking grey dust during the Breaking. It remains the most desolate region of the Outer Nations.

The Sunken Foundries: The legendary workshops of [Zareos](info?content=zareos) were physically buried by tectonic shifts during the gods' retreat. Many Spark Bearers believe that if these foundries could be reopened, the secrets of Sun-Iron would return to the world.
