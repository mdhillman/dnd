# Oriana

Often called The Cosmic Anchor or The Inevitable Embrace, Oriana represents the force of gravity and the crushing inevitability that draws like to like. She is the slow, heavy, and irresistible entity that yearns to collapse the universe back into a single point. While her counterpart, Zareos, seeks to explode existence outward with kinetic energy, Oriana is the force that keeps reality from flying apart into the void, forming stars from dust clouds not out of a desire for structure, but out of a mindless hunger for density.

In the cosmology of the Octad, Oriana is the glue that holds the material universe together. Without her, the energy of Zareos would scatter matter across infinite space into a meaningless, dilute soup. It is Oriana's immense, crushing will that forces atoms to cohere, forming the building blocks of reality, and that pulls celestial bodies into their stable orbits. She is not a benevolent guardian, but a force of pure, dispassionate necessity. Her ultimate desire, if left unchecked, would be to pull all of existence into a single, infinitesimally small and dense singularity, ending the universe in a "Great Crunch."

The stained-glass depictions in the High Sanctum show Oriana as a majestic and imposing female figure of immense power.

* **Appearance:** Her most striking feature is her hair, which is a flowing, cosmic vortex of deep blues, purples, and indigo glass segments, swirling with the patterns of galaxies, nebulae, and collapsing stars. Her eyes glow with a cold, detached violet light.
* **Attire:** She is clad in heavy, geometric armor made of dark iron, obsidian, and faceted deep blue glass shards, giving her a sense of immense, unyielding weight and solidity.
* **Attributes:** She is often shown surrounded by a swirling mosaic of gravitational fields, with planets and stars being pulled towards her, emphasizing her role as the center of all attraction.

## Worship and clergy

Clerics of Oriana are known as Anchors or Void Callers. They are a quiet, contemplative order of scholars, astronomers, and architects. They are rarely seen in the bustling centers of civilization, preferring places of silence, immense weight, and enduring structure.

Worship involves acts of patience, silence, and the study of celestial movements. "Weight-bearing" is a common form of devotion, where initiates must hold heavy stones for hours to understand the burden of the cosmos. Rituals are often performed underground or in massive, windowless observatories. Oriana's temples are not towering spires, but deep subterranean crypts, massive, fortified bunkers, or squat, heavy observatories built from the densest stone available, designed to endure for eternity.
