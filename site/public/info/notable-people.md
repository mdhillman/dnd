# Notable people

Behind every grand movement, industrial revolution, and shadow organization is a person whose ambition, madness, or sheer stubbornness changed the world. This index covers the political leaders, the titans of industry, and the legends whose names are whispered in both high courts and back alleys.

## Heads of State

* **High Arbiter Alistair Greatblood**: The face of the Concord is not a king, but an elderly man with ink-stained fingers and a reputation for being able to read three technical blueprints at once. Greatblood rose to power by successfully "re-zoning" the poorer districts of Revelstone without any of the nobility having to meet with a single peasant.

* **Lord Steffan Lyon**: The current High Lord and ruler of Camorr. A shrewd political veteran, he is the primary architect of Camorr’s modern industrial boom and a firm ally of Alistair Greatblood in the Great Council.

* **High King Aros II**: The current ruler of Doriath and the commander of the Sylvan Hosts. A stern man, known for his prowess in both leadership and combat, he is the embodiment of the "Old Oaths." While he has publicly disagreed with High Arbiter Alistair Greatblood on the merits of rapid expansion, his loyalty to the Concord is absolute.

* **High Druid Morrenna**: The spiritual and political leader of Andelain. She is a woman who is rumored to be over 150 years old, her life extended by her bond with the Great Oaks of Woodhelven. She is a calming, if enigmatic, presence to the people of Andelain.

* **Inquisitor Rhuarc**: Whilst the Silent Emporer is the de-facto head of state for New Mallorea, it's Rhuarc that holds the heart of the people. Rhuarc is the spiritual architect of the "New Order" in New Mallorea. He is not a traditional priest, but the head of the Cult of the Perfect Prime, a religion that treats mathematical logic as the only true divinity and the Old Empire as its lost, perfect paradise.

* **War-Chief Ghorza**: A Dwarven veteran of the Re-Indexing War, Ghorza leads the nomadic Union through sheer force of will and an uncanny ability to find water in the harshest deserts. Unlike most rulers, she lives in a mobile command-tent no different from her soldiers. She reportedly once head-butted a Gnomish walker until its pilot apologized for "interrupting her lunch."

## Titans of Industry & Celebrities

* **Cygnus "The Chaste" Lyon**: The High Lord Steffan's young son, whose nickname is a masterpiece of Camorrian irony. Cygnus is notoriously promiscuous and a permanent fixture in the scandal-sheets of the court, well-known to the younger male & female socialites of Lyonhall for his charm and his utter lack of interest in "chastity".

* **Lord Silas Sterling**: The CEO of the Cog-and-Hammer Consortium, Sterling is a man who believes that the soul is just a poorly-designed biological engine. He is responsible for the "Sterling-Standard" gear-teeth used in 90% of the world’s machinery. He is currently fighting a lawsuit from the Hearth-Mothers' Guild after his "Self-Cleaning Oven" accidentally gained sentience and tried to join their clergy.

* **"Glitter-Glint" Jinx**: A Kobold actor who has become a global sensation. Jinx is famous for her high-energy dramatic performances that are known to bring tears to the hardest of hearts. She is the face and spokesperson of the rising "Magic-Chic" fashion.

* **[Lyro Nox](/info?content=lyro-nox)**: Lyro Nox is widely regarded as the most gifted and popular Bard & performer of the current age, possessing a vocal range and tonal versatility deemed physically impossible by musical scholars. Lyro’s performances draw unparalleled crowds across the Kindred Concord, with critics noting the music often evokes intense, primal emotions that transcend mere entertainment.

* **[Thaddeus Thorne](/info?content=thaddeus-thorne)**: Thaddeus Thorne is the serving Commander of the [Tactical Hex Unit & Magical Policing (T.H.U.M.P)](/info?content=thump) organisation, a specialized division of city law enforcement tasked with high-risk arcane containment in major Theian metropolitan centers. Known for his imposing physical presence, profound cynicism regarding the arcane arts, and a near-supernatural ability to ignore magical effects through sheer stubbornness, Thorne is widely credited with codifying the modern doctrine of "Percussive Counterspelling".

## Notorious Criminals

* **The "Greasemonkey" Garret**: A disgraced artificer who nearly bankrupted the Silk & Shadow Syndicate by creating "Counterfeit Reality." He used complex illusions to make lead bars look and feel like gold, only for them to revert to lead forty-eight hours later—usually while inside a high-security vault. Incarcerated in the Vault of Silence (Nihilus’s Prison). He is allowed no tools, as he once tried to pick a lock using a piece of hardened sourdough bread.

* **Black-Eyed Beatrice**: The former leader of a sky-pirate fleet that preyed on Mallorean supply lines. She was known for her "Kala-Kissed" luck, surviving three separate shipwrecks before being brought down by a coordinated strike from the Aegis Blood-Blade. She was not executed, but "re-indexed." She now works as a highly-efficient (and deeply miserable) logistics clerk in the Halls of Consensus.

* **[Krell & Bane](/info?content=krell-and-bane)**: Barnaby Krell (Human, Necromancer) and Sylvania Bane (High-Elf, Sorcerer) were a couple defined by their volatile, passionate, and extremely profitable love of chaos. They tore through the northern territories for over a decade, treating every treasury and bank as a stage for their unique brand of magical anarchy.

## Legendary Heroes

* **Saint Faelen the Builder**: 
The legendary champion of Daesis. It is said Faelen didn't slay dragons with a sword; he built traps so complex the dragons simply gave up and moved to another continent. He is credited with constructing the original foundations of Camorr. Legend says he once "fixed" a volcano by installing a massive pressure-release valve, which now powers a very large, ancient brewery.

* **Elara the First-Light**: A hero of Zareos who famously held the "Gap of Sorrows" against an army of void-wraiths during The Breaking. She carried a shield made of solidified sunlight that was so bright it permanently bleached the surrounding canyon walls white. Elara didn't die in battle; she supposedly "ascended" when she forgot to turn her shield off, becoming a permanent, unmoving star that navigators still use today.

* **Lykos Lucien**: Lykos Lucien was the archetypal Paladin-Knight, often cited as the mortal bridge between the unyielding order of Oriana and the fierce protective light of Zareos. Known as the "Silver Ward," he was famous for his "Iron Vow" - a magical oath that rendered him physically immovable as long as he stood in defense of the innocent. The four nation states of Lykos still argue over the correct interpretation of his teachings to this day.
