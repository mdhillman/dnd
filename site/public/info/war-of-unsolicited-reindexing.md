# The War of the Unsolicited Re-Indexing

The War of the Unsolicited Re-Indexing (1157 - 1169 NA) was a twelve-year geopolitical tantrum initiated by the Old Mallorean Empire. It is primarily remembered today not for its grand cavalry charges or heroic last stands, but for being the single most expensive and violent attempt to fix a "perceived lack of administrative uniformity" in the South-East territories. The conflict directly resulted in the dissolution of the Old Mallorean Empire and the subsequent formation of The Kindred Concord roughly 250 years ago-an alliance between the predominantly human nations of Doriath, Andelain, and Camorr.

## Events of import

Whilst the war consisted of a twelve-year slog of assorted skirmishes, unfruitful negotiations, and name-calling, a number of key events find themselves the key research areas of today's scholars, historians, and freaky military-buffs.

### Origins: The Grand Census of Sovereignty

The war began in 1157 NA when the then Mallorean Emperor, Maximillian the Meticulous, decided that the varying tax codes, measurement units, and burial rites of his southern neighbors were "a direct insult to the structural integrity of logic." Located in the East, Mallorea shared a direct border with Camorr, viewing the bustling mercantile hub as a chaotic mess of unfiled receipts.

Mallorea deployed "Census Legions", armies of heavily armored bureaucrats supported by elite warmages, southward into the coastal territories. Their orders were to re-map every acre of the south east and annex any territory that failed to provide a notarized "Certificate of Existence" within three business days. The predominantly human nations of the south east, traditionally more interested in trade and local governance, found common ground in their shared hatred of Mallorean paperwork.

### Camorr: The First Breach

As the southern neighbor to Mallorea, Camorr bore the initial brunt of the "Administrative Invasion." A coastal mercantile republic, Camorr initially tried to bribe the invaders to go away. However, when the Malloreans attempted to "standardize" the Camorrian currency by melting it all down into gray lead cubes, the city’s merchant princes realized that war was significantly more profitable than being indexed.

### Andelain: The Iron Breadbasket

Located further into the south, past Camorr, Andelain provided the "muscle" of the resistance. A nation of hardy farmers and forest-folk, they famously held the sprawling border-forest and its vertical stronghold, the tree-top village of [Woodhelven](info?content=woodhelven), for six months. The Mallorean Census Legions found it mathematically impossible to calculate property taxes for residences that moved three inches to the left every time the wind blew, and the defense was allegedly fueled by nothing but spite, a tactical height advantage, and a particularly potent batch of potato moonshine.

### Doriath: The Arcane Archives

Doriath utilized its own specialized arcane traditions to resist. They specialized in "Bureaucratic Counter-Spelling," turning Mallorean maps into sentient, biting creatures and enchanting the invader's inkwells to only produce a garish pink ink and their quills to only write insults regarding the Emperor’s mother. Bordering the Spire's Exclusion Zone, Doriath benefitted from one of the most unapproachable borders during the war, although a Mallorean contigent did attempt to march through the zone, leading to the incident now known as The Great Blip.

### Beginning of the End: THe Great Blip

For centuries, Doriath has maintained the "Strictly No Loitering" policy around the Spire. They designated the area an Exclusion Zone not out of territorial greed, but because the Spire is less of a mountain and more of a geographical tantrum. Doriathian "Boundary Sentinels" (mostly wizards with high-powered telescopes and very long-range evacuation plans) spend their days ensuring that no one attempts to "tap" the Spire's power, as the mountain has a history of reacting to such attempts with extreme prejudice.

The Mallorean High Command, led by Arch-Calculator Vane, dismissed the Doriathian warnings as "superstitious inefficiency." They equipped the 4th Logic Legion with Ley-Line Stabilizers - colossal, brass-bound metaphysical "tent pegs" designed to hammer the chaotic energy of the Spire into a predictable, taxable grid. Vane’s plan was simple: "We shall march through the chaos, organize it, and arrive in Doriath with the laws of physics firmly under our jurisdiction."

In late 1168 NA, a Mallorean legion helmed by Vane himself entered the Exclusion Zone. As they began hammering their stabilizers into the ground, the Spire-which fundamentally operates on the principle of "Whatever is funniest or most inconvenient at the time", responded to the forced order with a localized reality-collapse known as The Great Blip.

The Spire did not simply vaporize the legion. Instead, it interpreted the Mallorean's desire for "orderly, uniform, and stationary placement" with cruel, literal precision. In a flash of violet static, five thousand soldiers were instantly transmuted into Ceramic Garden Gnomes. Every soldier was rendered into one of four approved poses (Standing, Pointing, Fishing, or Sleeping), each painted in vibrant, weather-resistant glazes. True to the Mallorean spirit, the only things that survived the blast were the legion's supply manifests, which were found neatly stacked atop the Arch-Calculator's now-ceramic head.

> "We watched through the scrying glass. One moment there was a phalanx of blue-skinned bureaucrats shouting about zoning laws; the next, there was just a very large, very quiet collection of lawn ornaments. It was the most tidy massacre in history." - Unamed Doriathian boundry sentinel.

### Wrapping it up: The Kindred Accord

The end of the war was signaled by the signing of the Kindred Accord in early 1170 NA.

This official conclusion was precipitated by the catastrophic Great Blip in 1168 NA, effectively "setting the military in stone" and ending Mallorea's ability to continue the conflict.

Following this, the transition to the current geopolitical stalemate was further defined by a "Total Administrative Blackout", as the remnants of Mallorea retreated behind the newly imposed demilitarised zone.

## Legacy

While the Concord celebrated, the remnants of Mallorea retreated behind a veil of secrecy and high-tensile red tape. Diplomatic relations with the New Mallorean government have been tense and sporadic over the last 250 years, few travellers are permitted (or brave enough) to venture through the DMZ to the country, so little is known about its current culture or geopolitical climate. Over the last few years, a cold war has begun to brew with both the Concord and New Mallorea testing eachother's border strength with small scale "military exercises" and the creation of more "signal intelligence" outposts along the DMZ.

More recently, in Andelain, suspected Mallorean agents have triggered "fermentation riots" by using complex, near-untracable potions to nullify the alcoholic content of some of the countries largest breweries and mead-houses.

Radicalized Mallorean specialists have also been known to act as magical suicide-bombers. They infiltrate Revelstone and intentionally "fail" cantrips to trigger detonations, leaving behind perfectly organized paperwork detailing the "regulatory failures" of the buildings they destroy.

> "Not only do they want to kill us; they want to prove that our lives are statistically irrelevant and poorly managed." - [Commander Thaddeus T. Thorne](info?content=thaddeus-thorne).
