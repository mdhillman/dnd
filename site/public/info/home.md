# Welcome to Theia!

Greetings, brave adventurers, and welcome to your new D&D home for the foreseeable future! This lore wiki is your definitive, though frequently unreliable, guide to the world of Theia (_thee-uh_). This campaign is a journey into the dark, fantastical, and down-write degenerate mind of your DM, me! For more details on how I plan to run the game, including what edition we'll be using, what sources we're pulling from, house rules and thoughts on play-styles, see the [From the DM](/info?content=rules-of-conduct) page.

## Theia in a nutshell

Theia consists of single large continental mass, also known as Theia, or "the main-land", roughly half the planet's surface; surrounded by countless smaller islands and, across the dark & uncharted seas, an immense, unknown void. Life here is vibrant, dangerous, and often deeply illogical. The usual rules of reality are more like gentle suggestions, guidelines if you will; easily ignored by a well-placed spell, the proper petition to one of the [Gods](/gods), or a trulyn relentless adventurer.

The continent is dominated by the monolithic [Spire](info?content=the-breaking) at its center. This mountain isn't just tall; it’s an active geographical feature that warps the environment around it. As one approaches, the very weave of magic becomes unstable, generating unpredictable, chaotic effects that frighten wizards and delight pyromancers. For this reason, travel into the Spire's inner territories is strictly forbidden - a rule that, naturally, is enforced with minimal success.

You will find that the [people of Theia](/info?content=races) are accustomed to the absurd. Local governance tends to be either terrifyingly oppressive or utterly nonexistent. Magical entities are commonplace, and powerful artifacts are often indistinguishable from slightly sticky kitchen implements. You'll encounter sprawling, chaotic cities where the crime lords are accountants and the most dangerous organization is the local town council.

Your journey will take you from the anarchic frontiers where bandits rule (sometimes through aggressive, loud performance art), to the few pockets of [civilization](/info?content=nations) clinging desperately to reason. You will deal with high-stakes larceny, existential threats, incompetent deities, and the inevitable inconvenience of misplaced paperwork.

Above all, remember this: the nature of Theia is fundamentally broken. Its thematic, and geographical, core is a cosmic maelstrom of wild magic. Keep your wits sharp, your sense of humour sharper, and try not to sign anything you haven't read - or, preferably, that doesn't spontaneously burst into flames the moment you touch it.

Good luck. You'll need it.
