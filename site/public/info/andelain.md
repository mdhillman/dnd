# Andelain

Andelain (officially the Grand Preserve of Andelain) is the mystical and agricultural heart of the Kindred Concord. Known for its sprawling ancient forests, floating "Skylands" and proximity to many ruins from the [Age of Legends](/info?content=the-age-of-legends), Andelain provides the alliance with its food, its magical scholars, and its most potent arcane defenses. While Camorr builds the engines of the New Age, Andelain ensures that the magic fueling them doesn’t unravel the fabric of reality.

## Geography and Major Settlements

### Glimmermere

The crown jewel of the west, Glimmermere is a sprawling city of white stone and living timber built along the shores of its similarly named lake. The city is famous for its "Reflective Spires," which are angled to catch the lake’s unique luminescence.

### Sea Reach

Perched on a craggy island just off the southern coast, Sea Reach serves as the primary maritime sentinel for Andelain, acting as a gateway for deep-sea trade and a harbor for the "Glass-Bottomed" fleets. The city is famous for its Salt-Warded Docks, which protect incoming wooden vessels from the corrosive magical static that occasionally rolls off the southern oceans.

### Millford

Nestled in the fertile valleys of the northeast, Millford is the undisputed breadbasket of the nation, dominated by massive, water-powered mills that grind "Sun-Wheat" into the glowing flour favored by the Concord’s elite. The town is a rhythmic sprawl of grinding stones and irrigation canals, where the air is perpetually thick with the sweet, golden dust of magically-enriched grain.

### Woodhelven

A world-famous architectural marvel situated on, and sprawling across, the border with Camorr. Woodhelven is a vertical, tree-top village built into the massive, magically-resilient timber of the border forests. During the War of the Unsolicited Re-Indexing, it served as a vital strategic holdout, as its shifting elevations made it a logistical nightmare for Mallorean surveyors.

## Notable Figures

* **High Druid Morrenna**: The spiritual and political leader of Andelain. She is a woman who is rumored to be over 150 years old, her life extended by her bond with the Great Oaks of Woodhelven. She is a calming, if enigmatic, presence to the people of Andelain.

* **Master Artificer Kaelen Vane**: A disgraced former scholar from the floating isles who now runs a "Repair & Hex" shop in the lower levels of Woodhelven. He is the Andelain's leading expert on Anti-Memetic Stabilization and is often consulted when T.H.U.M.P. encounters a "Thinking Artifact."

* **"Thistle" (The Green-Knight)**: A legendary ranger and folk hero who protects travelers in the deep woods. Clad in armor made of enchanted bark and iron-wood, Thistle is said to be able to step into one tree and emerge from another miles away.

* **Robin of the Reeds**: Known only by their moniker, Robin of the Reeds is a legendary outlaw who haunts the marshes and waterways between Glimmermere and Millford. Unlike traditional highwaymen, Robin targets the local Barons, Lords, and visiting Camorrian merchants who attempt to hoard Andelain’s natural resources for industrial export.

## Economy and Culture

In the Grand Preserve of Andelain, the clink of the Concord Sovereign is frequently drowned out, the true economy of the nation operates on a higher plane of exchange where arcane knowledge and tangible power are the primary denominations. To an Andelainian scholar or a Glimmermere merchant, a notarized scroll containing a lost ritual, a stabilized "Memory Crystal" from the deep mines, or a relic salvaged from the Age of Legends is worth more than a mountain of gold.

"In Camorr, they want to put the world in a box. In Andelain, we are simply happy the world hasn't decided to eat the box yet." — Andelainian proverb regarding industrialization.

* **The Weavers of the Veil**: The most powerful guild in the country, the Weavers are responsible for maintaining the "Magical Safety Wards" that prevent the Realmatic Flux from destabilizing the member nations. They act as the primary consultants for REDACTED based in Revelstone, though they officially deny any knowledge of the faction.

* **The Harvesters’ Circle**: A guild of magically-attuned farmers who utilize "Growth-Chants" to maximize crop yields. They control the export of Sun-Wheat and the rare "Phase-Berries" used in high-end Camorrian alchemy.

* **The Festival of the First Light**: An annual celebration where thousands of paper lanterns are released into the magical mists of the Aethel-Rill valley. It is believed that the lanterns carry messages to the "Echoes" of those lost during The Breaking.

* **The Silence Pact**: In Andelain, it is considered extremely rude to use "Loud Magic" (fireballs, thunder-claps) without a permit. The culture prizes subtle, "quiet" arcana that blends seamlessly with the natural world.
