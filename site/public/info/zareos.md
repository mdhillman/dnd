# Zareos

In the cosmology of the Eight, the universe began as a cold, static void of space filled with inert matter. Zareos is the entity that "happened" to the universe, igniting the first stars and setting the celestial bodies in motion. Theological scholars argue that Zareos is not merely fire, but the concept of "doing." Without him, the universe would be a frozen tableau; with him, it is a violent, churning engine.

Zareos is described as volatile, impatient, and overwhelmingly intense. He possesses a hatred for stagnation. To Zareos, a mountain is an offense because it sits still; he prefers the volcano because it acts. He is not inherently destructive, but his demand for constant transformation often looks like destruction to mortals. He does not speak in riddles but in commands, demanding action, reaction, and conversion of state.

While early primitive cultures depicted Zareos as a formless pillar of fire or a shapeless cloud of lightning, the modern orthodox church (and particularly the famous stained-glass windows of the [High Sanctum]()) depicts him as a fierce warrior.

* **Appearance:** He is shown with a voluminous, wild beard that flows like flame, representing the generation of energy through fire.
* **Attire:** He is clad in a suit of blindingly bright armor, sometimes interpreted as pristine, glowing plate mail, other times as a sharp, structured tunic that mimics the fashion of high nobility, symbolizing the purity of white light.
* **Attributes:** He is almost always grasping a jagged bolt of blue-gold lightning, not as a weapon of war, but as a tool or staff, suggesting he controls the power rather than simply unleashing it.

## Worship and clergy

Clerics of Zareos are known as Spark Bearers. They are rarely found in quiet monasteries. Instead, they are found in the noisy, dangerous places of the world: the foundries of smiths, the laboratories of alchemists, and the front lines of revolutions.

Worship involves the expenditure of energy. "Static prayer" is considered an insult. Rituals involve dancing to the point of exhaustion, the burning of effigies, or the loud hammering of metal. Zareos has few traditional temples. His "shrines" are great furnaces, steam engines, and places where magic is raw and untamed.
