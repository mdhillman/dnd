# The New Age

The New Age (also known as the Era of Gears, The Second Ascent, or The Industrial Reconciliation) is the current epoch of Theian history. It began approximately 1,426 years ago (at least by current calendar reckoning), following the long, primitive stagnation of The Breaking. It is defined by a pragmatic, often cynical, synthesis of early steam-power, clockwork engineering, and a strictly codified approach to magic. While the grand miracles of the Age of Legends remain out of reach, the New Age is characterized by the mortal races' stubborn refusal to remain in the dark.

## The Technological Renaissance

The hallmark of the New Age is the concept of "Re-discovery." Having spent millennia surviving on bronze and superstition, the scholars of the Kindred Concord and the engineers of the Bronze Union began to reverse-engineer the "Dead Tech" of the ancestors.

* **Early Steam & Coal**: The discovery of "High-Yield Anthracite" allowed for the creation of the first reliable steam engines. These lack the elegant, silent grace of the Eternal Engines, being instead loud, soot-spewing, and prone to "rhythmic structural failure."

* **The Boom-Stick**: Perhaps the most controversial invention of the last century is the firearm. Using a crude mix of sulfur, saltpeter, and trace amounts of Magical Static, these basic flintlock and matchlock weapons have begun to change the face of warfare, much to the chagrin of traditional Knightly Orders.

* **The Rail-Lines**: While limited in scope compared to the ancient networks, iron-clad steam trains now connect major trade hubs like Revelstone and Port Meticulous, though they must frequently stop to "de-gauss" from magical interference.

## The Modern Weave

In the New Age, magic is no longer a spiritual communion; it is a hazardous material. The Octad are distant, and their power is filtered through a world that is still "noisy" from The Breaking.

* **The Bureaucracy of Spells**: Organizations like T.H.U.M.P. ensure that magic is used within "Safe Tolerances." A wizard is often just as likely to reach for a magnifying glass or a slide-rule as they are a wand.

* **Andelain Archivists**: The magical scholars of Andelain follow strict process and procedure when testing magical devices and experimenting with long-forgotten spells. Their learnings are fiercly guarded and students must spend years proving they are trustworthy before they're given access.

* **REDACTED**: A growing, albeit secretive, branch of government dedicated to monitoring the increasing frequency of "Anti-Memetic" events — incidents where people, places, or memories simply cease to have ever existed.

## Cultural Landscape

The prevailing mood of the New Age is one of "Pragmatic Nihilism." Most citizens are aware that they live in the shadow of giants (the Age of Legends) and under the gaze of potentially indifferent gods.

* **The Ruin-Trade**: "Scav-Guilds" make a dangerous living trekking into Age of Legends ruins to recover "Sun-Iron" or "Resonant Glass," which are then sold to the highest bidder to be melted down or used as luxury paperweights.

* **The Spire-Watch**: The Spire remains the constant horizon-marker for all of Theia. While it was once a site of pilgrimage, it is now largely viewed as a colossal environmental hazard that ruins local weather patterns and makes compasses spin in circles.
