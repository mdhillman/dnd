# T.H.U.M.P

T.H.U.M.P, Tactical Hex Unit & Magical Policing (though often colloquially referred to as "The Heavies", "The Door Knockers", or "Oh Gods No, Not Them"), is a specialized, high-impact sub-division of municipal law enforcement forces in major metropolitan centers across Theia, most notably cities with high magical activity like XXX or XXX. The organisation is lead by [Commander Thaddeus T Thorne](/info?content=thaddeus-thorne), who is widely credited with codifying the modern doctrine of "Percussive Counterspelling".

Unlike standard arcane investigators who utilize divination rituals and complex counter-spells, T.H.U.M.P units are deployed for "decisive kinetic intervention" in high risk magical situations where subtlety has failed or is deemed contractually unnecessary. Their primary mandate is the physical disruption of spellcasting engaged in by hostile entities, usually through the application of overwhelming blunt force trauma.

## History & formation

The need for a unit like T.H.U.M.P arose during later years of [The New Age](), as urban populations grew denser and the ambient magical background radiation of Theian cities increased. Traditional city guards, armed with standard issue chainmail and halberds, found themselves woefully under-equipped to handle "Code Magenta" incidents, such as spontaneous polymorph riots or unlicensed Lich activity in residential zoning areas.

The catalyzing event for the formation of the inaugural T.H.U.M.P unit is widely considered to be the "Vanderratz Tower Incident" of 1384 NA. An, arguably unhinged, Transmutation specialist, angry about rising property taxes, sealed himself in his tower with a powerful Globe of Invulnerability spell and began turning passing tax collectors into decorative lawn flamingos. Because magic could not penetrate the tower to neutralize him, the City Council authorized a simpler approach. A squad of decommissioned Warforged and three particularly large Hill Trolls were hired to simply push the tower over.

The operation was deemed a success (despite the complete destruction of three adjacent city blocks), and the doctrine of "Physical Resistance to Arcane Nonsense" was established.

## Methodology & tactics

T.H.U.M.P operates on a unique principle of magical theory known as Corporeal Density Negation. The theory posits that if an object (or officer) is sufficiently dense, heavy, and intellectually incurious, they possess a natural resistance to magical effects.

Pre-approved tactics are straightforward and rarely deviate from the field manual:
* **Breach and Clear:** units do not pick locks. They remove the wall containing the door. This often disorients the hostile spellcaster, interrupting their concentration.
* **Percussive Maintenance:** Used against magical constructs or stubborn wizards. This involves hitting the target repeatedly with a standardized "Compliance Hammer" until it stops glowing.
* **The Bear Hug**: The primary method for neutralizing a caster using somatic (hand gesture) components. An officer of sufficient girth wraps their arms around the suspect and squeezes until the suspect’s ribs prevent adequate movement.

T.H.U.M.P officers generally do not utilize counter-spells or protective wards, as these often interfere with the natural magic resistance provided by being made of stone or having a skull four inches thick.

## Composition and recruitment

Recruitment for T.H.U.M.P is based almost exclusively on physical fortitude, lifting capacity, and a distinct lack of imagination. High intelligence or wisdom scores are generally considered grounds for disqualification, as an officer who stops to ponder the ethical implications of an Eldritch Blast is an officer who is about to be vaporized.

Typical recruits include:

* **Hill and Mountain Trolls:** Prized for regeneration and reach, though they require hazard pay for daylight shifts.
* **Constructs (Golems/Warforged):** Usually surplus military units re-fitted with police lights and restraining bolts.
* **Dwarves:** Specifically those kicked out of mining guilds for excessive use of explosives.
* **Humans:** Only those possessing exceptional constitution and a history of surviving severe head trauma.

## Standard equipment

T.H.U.M.P gear is designed for weight rather than mobility. Their armor is often lead-lined plate, triple-thick, designed less to stop swords and more to prevent the officer from being levitated away by a gentle breeze.

They carry no magical items other than Standard Issue Manacles (Size Category: Large to Gargantuan) and the Breaching Maul, a hammer often weighing in excess of 80 pounds, used for "opening dialogue" with fortified doors.

## Controversy

T.H.U.M.P is frequently criticized by the majority of local Mage Guilds (as well as the national [Theian Mages Guild]()) and various civil liberty groups for excessive force and catastrophic property damage. The common complaint is that while T.H.U.M.P always apprehends the criminal spellcaster, they often destroy the building the offender was in, as well as the two neighboring buildings, during the arrest. A famous headline in the XXX read: "T.H.U.M.P Saves Neighborhood from Fireball... by Flattening Neighborhood".
