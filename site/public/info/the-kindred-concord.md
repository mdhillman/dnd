# The Kindred Concord

The Kindred Concord (often shortened to The Concord) is a sovereign geopolitical alliance of three predominantly human-governed nations of the lower continent: Camorr, Andelain, and Doriath. Established in 1170 NA, the alliance serves as the stabilizing force of the lower continent, dominating the global economy, maritime trade, and the advancement of steam-based technologies. The Concord is governed by the Great Council from the neutral city-state of Revelstone.

## Member Nations

[Camorr](/info?content=camorr) (The Commercial Heart): The maritime and manufacturing powerhouse. Camorr provides the Concord with its naval strength and the lion’s share of its steam-engine production. It is a land of merchants, industrialists, and the "gentlemanly" criminal underworld.

[Andelain](/info?content=andelain) (The Arcane Preserve): The mystical and agricultural backbone. Andelain manages the delicate balance of the Realmatic Flux and serves as the primary site for the study of Age of Legends ruins. It provides the Concord with its scholars, its food, and its most potent magical defenses.

[Doriath](/info?content=doriath) (The Ancient Shield): A tradition-bound woodland kingdom. Doriath maintains the alliance's land borders and provides the most elite scouts and rangers to the Revelstone Militia. While often at odds with Camorr’s smoky industrialism, Doriath’s commitment to the "Old Oaths" makes them the alliance’s most loyal defenders.

## History

The Concord was born from a twelve-year "geopolitical tantrum" thrown by the Old Mallorean Empire. Seeking to fix a "perceived lack of administrative uniformity" in the South-East, the Mallorean Emperor, Maximillian the Meticulous, launched an invasion to standardize the tax codes and burial rites of his neighbors.

### The Administrative Invasion

The war was characterized by the deployment of "Census Legions" - armies of armored bureaucrats who annexed any territory that failed to provide a notarized "Certificate of Existence" within three business days.

* Camorr’s Resistance: Initially attempting to bribe the invaders, the merchant princes of Camorr revolted when Mallorea attempted to melt their currency into uniform lead cubes.
* The Spite of Andelain: Protected by the vertical stronghold of the great forests, the herd-tenders and farmers of Andelain held the line. The Mallorean invaders found it mathematically impossible to tax property that moved every time the wind blew, while the defenders were fueled by "potato moonshine" and sheer spite.
* Doriath’s Counter-Bureaucracy: Doriathian mages enchanted Mallorean quills to write only insults about the Emperor's mother, turning the invader's own paperwork against them.

### The Great Blip (1168 NA)

The conflict reached its surreal climax when Mallorean Arch-Calculator Vane attempted to "organize" the Spire's chaotic energy using Ley-Line Stabilizers. The Spire responded with a reality-collapse that instantly transmuted five thousand soldiers into Ceramic Garden Gnomes. Rendered into four approved poses (Standing, Pointing, Fishing, or Sleeping), the "Gnome Legion" stands to this day as the most tidy massacre in history.

### The Signing of the Accord (1170 NA)

With their military literally set in stone, Mallorea retreated. The three victim nations (Camorr, Andelain, and Doriath) signed the Kindred Accord, vowing to maintain their shared independence and ensuring that no one would ever again attempt to "index" their sovereignty.

## Governance and Influence

The Concord is officially led by the Great Council in Revelstone, currently presided over by High Arbiter Alistair Greatblood.

* The Unity Currency: The "Concord Sovereign" (a brass-and-silver coin with a lead core to prevent magical counterfeiting) is the primary currency used in international trade.
* Technological Hegemony: Through the control of Steel refineries and patent laws, the Concord ensures that any nation seeking "Early Industrial" level technology must do so through their trade routes.
* Bureaucratic Peace: The Concord is famous for its "Paper Shield" - a vast network of treaties and red tape that makes declaring war so administratively difficult that most nations simply give up and go to the pub instead.

## Foreign Relations

While the Concord is the dominant force in Theia, it faces constant pressure from:

* New Mallorea: The isolationist rival to the north whose "Quietist" philosophy is viewed as a direct threat to the Concord’s loud, busy industrialism.
* The Minor Inner Nations: A collection of independent bronze-age city-states and nomadic tribes who view the Concord as arrogant "Gear-Worshipers".
* The Outer Nations: A wide spectrum of other large countries, spread across the contintent, each vying for their position as the strongest countries with the longest legacies by any means possible.
* REDACTED: While technically sanctioned by the Council, the REDACTED operates with a level of secrecy that leaves the Concord’s own diplomats in the dark about what reality-ending threat was neutralized last Tuesday.
