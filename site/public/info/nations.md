# Nations of Theia

The world of Theia is a tapestry woven from countless sovereign territories, kingdoms, and independent city-states, each a reflection of the rich diversity of its inhabitants. Far from a unified political entity, Theia is a complex geopolitical landscape shaped by centuries of migration, conflict, and cooperation. Its nations are derived from the foundational cultures and traditions of all major races—from the stout, mountain-dwelling Dwarves to the ethereal Elves of ancient forests, the fiercely communal Orcs, and the extremely adaptable Human populations.

These varied states range from sprawling empires and secretive oligarchies to humble tribal lands and bustling mercantile republics, creating a vibrant, often contentious, mosaic of political life across the planet.
