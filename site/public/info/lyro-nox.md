# Lyro Nox

Lyro Nox is widely regarded as the most gifted and popular Bard & performer of the current age, possessing a vocal range and tonal versatility deemed physically impossible by musical scholars. Lyro’s performances draw unparalleled crowds across the XXX, with critics noting the music often evokes intense, primal emotions that transcend mere entertainment.

Lyro is famous for performing duets, trios, and even full choir pieces entirely solo, flawlessly switching between tempos and registers. Many listeners claim to recognize specific, distinctive tones—such as the "bellow of a seasoned Dwarven sailor", the "delicate sigh of an ancient Celestial", or the "high pitched wailing of a Banshee in flight". The fact each can come from a single person seems to defy all known acoustic physics. 

Known for their striking, almost unsettling beauty and androgynous presentation, Lyro maintains a carefully cultivated aura of mystery. Lyro rarely grants interviews, preferring the focus to remain on the purity and power of their performances.

## Unexplained phenomena and rumours

Despite Lyro's wide-reaching fame, their origins and the source of their impossible talent are subjects of relentless speculation, among the academic and arcane communities as well as the house-wives of the upper nobility.

Lyro's performances are often followed by severe exhaustion and fits of deep existential melancholy or manic obsession with organizational tasks. This fuels the gossip that Lyro's body is merely a fragile vessel for the energies of entities too numerous and complex for a single mind to contain.

## Present day relevance

Lyro Nox remains an enigma. Though politically neutral, their massive popularity grants them access to courts, palaces, and forbidden locales. They are known to accept commissions for performances that seem strategically pointless, such as singing for the lower-class miners near old, abandoned quarries or staging a massive concert amoungst the ruins of a long-deserted castle. These peculiar choices lead many to believe Lyro is not seeking fame or fortune, but rather reaching for a specific note or seeking a specific, powerful voice; a final piece in the cosmic symphony that only they can hear.
